# Grounding — digests only

This folder holds the component digests (*-digest.md) the adapter was built
from: the RZL index, RbotLoadR, RGENERATR, RGHOSTCHAIN, RGHOSTKEY, RWALET,
RTRACKR, RMAIL, and AUTOMANUAL summaries.

The raw component files themselves stay local (on Neo and in the Muse
workspace): they include binaries, multi-MB archives, and private identifiers
that do not belong in a public repo. Two values were redacted from the pushed
rmail-digest.md only: the 17e UDID and the ghost@ mailbox address. The local
digest keeps them.

Nothing here is a secret. The adapter is read-only against the chain and the
wallet, and it never signs, seals, enrolls, or mints.
