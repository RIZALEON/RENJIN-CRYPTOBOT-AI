▸ THE LOOP — HOW A QUESTION TRAVELS

From corpus line 20: “The loop order is access, gate, tokenizer, mind, heart, spine, law, mouth.” Every stage runs on the device.

ACCESS
Reads text, file, bio and speech when the host allows it.
GATE
Checks the ЯID and the chain before any work.
TOKENIZER
Turns bytes into ids and ids back into bytes. tok.v1, vocab 313.
MIND
Recalls lessons, laws and the chain — BM25 + RZL embeddings.
HEART
Continues a context with greedy deterministic bytes.
SPINE
Acts first, speaks only from real results. Every claim is quoted.
LAW
Blocks a hard no and records the refusal on the chain.
MOUTH
Speaks in the language of the user.

▸ HONEST LIMITS

What the model still cannot do — from its own build logs

  • It cannot answer a free question in its own words. Greedy generation returns 30 spaces. Sampled at temperature 0.8 it returns gibberish fragments — "er teeen yhee csadto eetrs sa". Retrieval-first quoting is the askable path for now.
  • The two grounded misses are fixed: corpus line 20 now reads “The loop order is access, gate, tokenizer, mind, heart, spine, law, mouth.” and line 103 says “Loss on the held-out lines…” instead of “held-out set” — the grounded test now passes 10/10.
  • Held-out loss barely moved (3.138 vs v2's 3.15) despite 10× the training time — the next step is better generation and retrieval, not more seconds.
  • All results are from a VM copy in Node with an in-memory store — not the on-device ЯENJIN. This page is not paired to a live device.
  • No wallet, exchange or background runner exists in this build. Paper trading comes before live trading, backtesting first — and a cryptobot never signs on its own.
